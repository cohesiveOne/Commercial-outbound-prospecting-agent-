# Cohesive Components — approved facts and claims

> Every playbook and every outreach draft may use only the facts on this page and in the
> segment playbook. Source: the Cohesive corporate deck (COH-004, v08-2026) and sales
> leadership (2026-10-14). If a claim isn't here, don't make it. Ask instead.

## Who we are

**Cohesive Components** solves complex converting challenges. We turn engineered materials into
high-performing, production-ready components with expertise, speed, and seamless execution.

We sit between the **material manufacturers** (who produce at scale) and **OEMs** (who need the
exact part). Our Applications Engineering team brings material expertise, application insight,
processing and engineering, and supplier relationships to bridge that gap.

## Speed

- **Prototypes in 2 business days** for materials we have in stock. Always include the
  "in-stock materials" qualifier.

## Applications Engineering and in-house testing

The engineering team helps with material selection, prototyping, tear-downs, alternate-material
testing and performance validation *before* the customer commits to production.

- **Mechanical testing:** tensile and elongation, tear strength, compression-deflection force,
  tension set, compression set, flexural modulus, hardness (Shore A, OO), density,
  **UL 94 burn testing**, and PSA peel force.
- **Specialty testing:** FTIR, **thermal conductivity**, **thermal impedance**, color
  temperature, **surface and volume resistivity**, UV-C aging, and cell structure microscopy.

## Never say

- **The customer's name in the CDU story.** Never write it, hint at it, or describe it so it
  could be identified. `config/icp.yaml → outreach_guardrails.banned_terms` enforces this.
- Ownership or financial details, or sales mix by market.
- "ITAR registered." The approved wording is **ITAR compliant**. ITAR applies to US locations
  only. Never say "all locations" or imply that Mexico or China is covered.
- That Mexico or Shanghai manufactures parts. They are 3PL warehouses and a sourcing office.
- That Cohesive holds a UL listing or recognition. We *test* to UL 94 in-house. Flame ratings
  belong to the material.
- Fluid-compatibility or wear-time guarantees. Offer to test and validate instead.
- A site-specific certification as if it covered every site. For example, AS9100 is Charlotte
  only, and ISO 13485 is Grand Rapids only.
