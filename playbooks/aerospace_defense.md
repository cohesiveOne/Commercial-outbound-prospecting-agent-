# Playbook: Aerospace, Satellite & Defense

> The outreach-writer may use only the claims in this file. Items marked `[CONFIRM]` must be

> registration, a security clearance, or program involvement that is not confirmed here.

## Who we sell to

OEMs and tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers that build satellites and spacecraft, launch vehicles,
defense electronics, avionics, radar, rugged computing, UAVs, eVTOL aircraft, and aircraft
interiors and aerostructures.

## Why now in this market

- Satellite constellations and defense modernization are driving rate production at companies
  that used to build in low volume.
- Smaller, denser electronics put more stress on EMI shielding and thermal management.
- Supply chain resilience mandates push primes and tier-1s to qualify domestic second sources.

## Applications to name

| Sub-segment | Components |

## Discovery questions

1. Which shielding or thermal parts are on long lead times or single-sourced today?
2. Are you moving any programs from prototype to rate production in the next 12 months?
3. Which material specs apply (outgassing, flammability, MIL-DTL)?
4. What does supplier qualification look like: FAI, source inspection, flow-down clauses?

## Objections

- **"Qualifying a new supplier takes too long."** We start with the build-to-print parts that
  have the least qualification burden, or with a new program before its design freezes.
- **"We need ITAR compliance."** Answer only with the confirmed status above. If it is not
  confirmed, route the question to sales leadership.
