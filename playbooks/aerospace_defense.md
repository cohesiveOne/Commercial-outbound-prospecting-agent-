# Playbook: Aerospace, Satellite & Defense

> Use only the claims in this file and in `_company.md`. Say **"ITAR compliant"**, never
> "ITAR registered". Never imply a security clearance or program involvement.

## Who we sell to

OEMs and tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers that build satellites and spacecraft, launch vehicles,
defense electronics, avionics, radar, rugged computing, UAVs, eVTOL aircraft, and aircraft
interiors and aerostructures.

## Why now in this market

- Satellite constellations and defense modernization are driving rate production at companies
  that used to build in low volume.
- Smaller, denser electronics put more stress on EMI shielding and thermal management.
- Supply-chain resilience pushes primes and tier-1s to qualify domestic second sources.

## Applications to name

| Sub-segment | Components |
|---|---|
| Satellite / space | EMI/RFI shielding gaskets · thermal pads and graphite heat spreaders for avionics · vibration-damping die-cuts · low-outgassing materials (validated per program) |
| Defense electronics / avionics | Conductive elastomer EMI gaskets · environmental seals · thermal interface materials · connector and enclosure gaskets |
| Aircraft / UAV | Flame-rated seals (material-rated, UL 94 tested in-house) · lightweight foam components · panel and harness gaskets |

## Proof points (confirmed)

- **AS9100 certified** at our Charlotte, NC site. **ISO 9001:2015** at all locations.
- **ITAR compliant** at our US locations (Charlotte NC, Cincinnati OH, Harrison OH, Grand
  Rapids MI). Do not extend this to Mexico or China.
- **EMI and thermal material partners:** Parker Chomerics (EMI shielding), Rogers, NeoGraf
  (graphite thermal management), 3M. Saint-Gobain and Novagard (sealants and silicones) are
  available when a spec calls for them.
- **In-house testing:** surface and volume resistivity (EMI), thermal conductivity and thermal
  impedance, UL 94 burn testing, compression set, and FTIR.
- **Prototypes in 2 business days** for in-stock materials. Useful for new programs and
  fast-turn spares.
- **Precision processes:** water jet, flat bed laser and laser rotary cutting, plus assembly and
  kitting.

## Discovery questions

1. Which shielding or thermal parts are on long lead times or single-sourced today?
2. Are any programs moving from prototype to rate production in the next 12 months?
3. Which material specs apply (outgassing, flammability, MIL-DTL)?
4. What does supplier qualification look like for you: FAI, source inspection, flow-downs?

## Objections

- **"Qualifying a new supplier takes too long."** Start with build-to-print parts that carry
  the least qualification burden, or with a new program before its design freezes.
- **"We need ITAR handling."** Our US locations are ITAR compliant, and controlled work stays
  in the US. For AS9100 work, that's our Charlotte, NC site.
