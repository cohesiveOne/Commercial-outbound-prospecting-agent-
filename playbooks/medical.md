# Playbook: Medical Devices (Wearables · Advanced Wound Care)

> Use only the claims in this file and in `_company.md`. Never make clinical, regulatory or
> wear-time claims.

## Who we sell to

- **Wearables:** OEMs of CGMs, insulin patch pumps and on-body injectors, ECG and biosensor
  patches, and remote patient monitoring wearables.
- **Wound care:** manufacturers of advanced dressings (foam, silicone, hydrocolloid, island),
  NPWT systems, and ostomy and skin-interface products.

Hospitals, clinics and distributors are not targets.

## Why now in this market

- Wear times are getting longer. Adhesive performance and skin comfort are top design
  concerns.
- Miniaturized devices need multi-layer laminated constructions with precise registration.
- Moving from clinical builds to commercial volume needs a converter that can scale under a
  medical quality system.

## Proof points (confirmed)

- **ISO 13485 certified, with cleanroom converting and controlled white room converting**, at
  our Grand Rapids, MI site.
- **Direct access to medical-grade foams and adhesives.** Partners include 3M and Avery Dennison.
- **Rotary (19) and laser rotary (3) die cutting plus 9 laminating lines** for multi-layer
  constructions, from prototype to volume.
- **Prototypes in 2 business days** for in-stock materials.
- **In-house testing:** PSA peel force, tensile and elongation, tear strength, density, cell
  structure microscopy, and FTIR material identification.
- **Value-add services:** assembly, bagging, packaging and kitting.

Biocompatibility data (ISO 10993) comes from the material suppliers. Refer to it that way, and
never as a Cohesive claim.

## Discovery questions

1. How long a wear time are you designing for, and where does adhesion fail today?
2. How many layers are in the on-body stack, and who converts them now?
3. What documentation does your supplier quality team need from a converter?
4. When does the next build move from pilot to commercial volume?

## Objections

- **"Changing converters means re-validation."** Start with a new program, a next-generation
  design, or a second source qualified in parallel.
- **"Our adhesive supplier converts for us."** We work with the same material suppliers and
  add multi-layer lamination, peel testing, kitting and DFM support, in a cleanroom under
  ISO 13485.
