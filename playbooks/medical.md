## Who we sell to

- **Wearables:** OEMs of CGMs, insulin patch pumps and on-body injectors, ECG and biosensor
  patches, and remote patient monitoring wearables.
- **Wound care:** manufacturers of advanced dressings (foam, silicone, hydrocolloid, island),
  NPWT systems, and ostomy and skin-interface products.

Hospitals, clinics and distributors are not targets.

## Why now in this market

- Wear times are stretching to 10–15 days. Adhesive failure and skin irritation are leading
  causes of complaints.
- Device miniaturization drives multi-layer laminated constructions that need precise
  registration.
- Companies moving from clinical builds to commercial volume often need a converter that can
  scale without re-validating the design.

## Discovery questions

1. What wear time are you designing for, and where does adhesion fail today?
2. How many layers are in the on-body stack, and who converts them now?
3. What documentation does your supplier quality team need (DHR inputs, change notification)?
4. When does the next build move from pilot to commercial volume?

## Objections

- **"Changing converters means re-validation."** We start with new programs or next-generation
  designs, or with a second source qualified in parallel.
- **"Our adhesive supplier converts for us."** We work with the same material suppliers and
  add multi-layer lamination, kitting and DFM support.
