## Who we sell to

OEMs that **design and build** data center thermal and containment hardware: CDU makers,
liquid-cooling and cold-plate companies, chiller and CRAH/CRAC manufacturers, and
containment, rack and enclosure builders. We do **not** sell to colocation operators or
hyperscalers. Those companies buy the finished equipment.

## Why now in this market

- AI and GPU racks above 50–100 kW are pushing direct-to-chip liquid cooling into mainstream
  deployments. CDU and manifold makers are adding capacity quickly and qualifying second sources.
- Higher densities raise the cost of a leak. Seal and gasket fluid compatibility with PG25,
  water-glycol and dielectric fluids now gets scrutiny at the design stage.
- Enclosures and containment systems need UL 94 flame-rated materials, so material
  substitutions trigger re-testing.

## Discovery questions

1. Which seals or gaskets on the CDU or manifold have caused leaks or fluid-compatibility problems?
2. Are you single-sourced on any gasket or TIM for the new high-density platform?
3. What flame rating do enclosure materials have to meet, and who owns that spec?
4. How are you handling the ramp: kitting, inventory programs, or second sources?

## Objections

- **"We already have a gasket supplier."** Most OEMs qualify a second source for capacity
  ramps. We can quote your current print side by side with no change to the drawing.
- **"Materials are specified by engineering."** Good. We'll work from the specified material
  and offer an alternative only if it reduces cost or lead time.
