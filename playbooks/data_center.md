# Playbook: Data Center Infrastructure (CDU · Chillers · Aisle Containment)

> Use only the claims in this file and in `_company.md`. Never name the customer in the CDU
> reference story.

## Who we sell to

OEMs that **design and build** data center thermal and containment hardware: CDU makers,
liquid-cooling and cold-plate companies, chiller and CRAH/CRAC manufacturers, and
containment, rack and enclosure builders. We do **not** sell to colocation operators or
hyperscalers. Those companies buy the finished equipment.

## Why now in this market

- AI and GPU racks are pushing direct-to-chip liquid cooling into mainstream deployments.
  CDU and manifold makers are adding capacity and qualifying second sources.
- Chilled-water and coolant loops run below the dew point of the room. **Condensation on
  chilled pipes inside CDUs** is a real design problem, and insulation has to be engineered
  to fit tight, complex pipe routing.
- Enclosures and containment systems have to meet UL 94 flammability requirements, so
  material substitutions trigger re-testing.

## Discovery questions

1. Where do you fight condensation inside the CDU or chiller today, and how is that
   insulation made and installed?
2. Are you single-sourced on any gasket, insulation or TIM part for the new high-density
   platform?
3. Which flammability rating do enclosure materials have to meet, and who owns that spec?
4. Would kitting the foam parts per unit simplify your assembly line?

## Objections

- **"We already have a gasket or insulation supplier."** Most OEMs qualify a second source
  during capacity ramps. We'll quote your current print side by side, with no change to the
  drawing.
- **"Materials are specified by engineering."** Good. We'll work from the specified material
  and can test an alternative in-house only if it reduces cost or lead time.
- **"Insulation is installed by hand from sheet or tube stock."** That's exactly the problem
  our CDU reference customer had. Die-cut, kitted insulation fits the pipe routing and cuts
  assembly time.
